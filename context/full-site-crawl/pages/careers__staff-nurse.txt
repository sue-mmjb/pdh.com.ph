Staff Nurse
Qualification:
We are seeking a compassionate and dedicated Staff Nurse to provide high-quality patient care in a fast-paced healthcare environment. Responsibilities include monitoring patient health, administering medications, coordinating with the healthcare team, and ensuring a safe, supportive atmosphere. The ideal candidate is empathetic, detail-oriented, and committed to patient well-being and professional excellence.
Job Description
1. Gives direct nursing care.
2. Identifies nursing needs of patients.
3. Plans, gives and evaluate nursing care.
4. Admits patient.
5. Performs such nursing activities such as bathing and oral hygiene.
6. Assists patient in moving, maintaining proper alignment of patient’s body and utilizing good body mechanic.
7. Takes and record vital sign.
8. Observes medical sepsis.
9. Observes signs and symptoms, institutes remedial action when appropriate and record these in her chart.
10. Report observations to the physician.
11. Observes and responds to patients’ emotional spiritual need.
12. Acts as liaison between patients and hospital personnel.
13. Interprets to the patient and his family their roles in promoting successful therapy and rehabilitation.
14. Prepares patients for and assists physicians with diagnostics and therapeutic procedure.
15. Carries out doctor’s orders.
16. Assist physicians when examining patients.
17. Performs irrigations, catheterizations and gives enema.
18. Administers and charts medication and patients’ reaction to medications.
19. Instruct patients and families.
20. Sees to it that equipment receives proper care maintenance.
21. Discharge patients.
22. Performs post- mortem care.
23. Teaches and direct new professional nursing personnel.
24. Acts as head nurse when so delegated.
25. Receive and endorses ward equipment and supplies.
26. Interpret hospital policies and procedures.
27. Gives health teaching.
28. Maintains good relationship with other hospital personnel.
29. Charts accurately medications given.
30. Attends meetings and conferences.
31. Is subject to cross training based on performance evaluation, and once assigned in the special area (OR-DR, NICU, ICU, PICU and ER) they shall observe whatever responsibilities, policies and procedures to be carried out for patient care that is imposed on that certain department under PCS.
32. To do other duties or activities that may be assigned by your superior from time to time.
