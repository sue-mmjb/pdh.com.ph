Keeping Hearts Healthy: PDH’s Cardiovascular Health Talk Brings Awareness to the Community
On March 20, 2025, Parañaque Doctors Hospital (PDH) took a proactive step toward heart health awareness by hosting an insightful Cardiovascular Health Talk, led by Dr. Paul Quetua, Internal Medicine Specialist. The event aimed to equip the community with lifesaving knowledge on preventing cardiovascular diseases, debunking common heart health myths, and promoting a heart-healthy lifestyle.
A United Effort for Healthier Hearts
This event wouldn’t have been a success without the unwavering support of Barangay Marcelo Green and Barangay Don Bosco, whose leaders showed their dedication to promoting health and wellness.
Barangay Marcelo Green Shows Their Heart!
A huge shoutout to Barangay Captain Ian V. Aguilar, Ma’am Myrna Aguilar, Health Committee Head Sir Menandro “Popoy” Molina, and Executive Secretary Ma’am Nicole Lirazan for their active participation. Their presence underscored their commitment to ensuring their community understands the importance of heart disease prevention.
Barangay Don Bosco Cares for Their Hearts!
We also extend our gratitude to Barangay Captain Mar Jimenez and Barangay Secretary Ma’am Milagros “Miles” Cruz, who, alongside their community members, embraced this opportunity to learn more about heart health. With the right knowledge, they are now better equipped to make healthier choices for themselves and their families.
More Than Just a Health Talk—It Was a Celebration!
At PDH, we believe that learning should be fun and engaging! That’s why we didn’t just share important heart health tips—we also made sure our participants had a great time!
Seeing the smiles and energy of our attendees proved that health education can be both meaningful and enjoyable!
Prevention Starts with Awareness
Cardiovascular diseases remain one of the leading causes of illness worldwide—but with the right knowledge and lifestyle choices, they are largely preventable. At PDH, we stand by the belief that prevention is the best cure—and it starts with empowering communities through accessible health education.
Thank you to everyone who joined us! Together, let’s keep our hearts strong and our communities healthier.
Kalingang ramdam, serbisyong matibay — ‘yan ang PDH!
