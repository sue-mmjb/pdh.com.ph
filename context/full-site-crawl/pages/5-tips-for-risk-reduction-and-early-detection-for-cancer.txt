5 Tips for Risk Reduction and Early Detection for Cancer
February marks Cancer Awareness Month, a time to shed light on the importance of early detection, prevention, and treatment. At Parañaque Doctors Hospital (PDH), we are committed to providing top-notch medical care and diagnostic services to help our community stay informed and proactive about their health.
Here are FIVE ESSENTIAL TIPS to help you reduce your risk and detect cancer early:
- Make Regular Check-Ups a Priority Early detection is key to successful treatment. Regular consultations with your doctor and routine screenings—such as mammograms, Pap smears, colonoscopies, and prostate exams—help identify potential health issues before they become serious.
At PDH, we offer comprehensive cancer screening services, including Ultrasound, and CT Scans. Our expert radiologists ensure accurate and timely results.
- Maintain a Healthy Lifestyle A balanced diet and regular exercise play a crucial role in cancer prevention. Consume more fruits, vegetables, and whole grains while reducing processed and red meats.
PDH’s Nutrition and Dietetics provides personalized meal planning and counseling to help you make healthier food choices.
- Avoid Smoking and Excessive Alcohol Consumption Smoking is a leading cause of lung cancer, while excessive alcohol intake is linked to liver, breast, and other cancers. Quitting smoking and drinking in moderation can lower your risk.
- Stay Sun-Safe Skin cancer is one of the most preventable types of cancer. Protect yourself by using sunscreen, wearing protective clothing, and avoiding prolonged sun exposure, especially during peak hours.
If you notice unusual skin changes, PDH’s Dermatology Department is here to provide expert consultations and treatment.
- Know Your Family History A family history of cancer can increase your risk. Inform your doctor about any hereditary conditions so they can recommend genetic counseling and early detection strategies.
PDH’s Oncology Consultations are here to assess hereditary cancer risks and create personalized health plans.
Why Choose Parañaque Doctors Hospital? At PDH, we prioritize your health by providing reliable diagnostic, treatment, and wellness services. Our dedicated team of specialists is here to guide you every step of the way.
