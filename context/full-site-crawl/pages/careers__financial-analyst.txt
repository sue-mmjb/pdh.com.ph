Financial Analyst
Qualification:
Bachelor of Science in Accountancy or Business-Related course and Should have 1-3 years’ experience as Accounting Assistant.
Job Description
- Provide timely, relevant and accurate reporting and analysis of the results of the hospital and departmental performance against historical, budgeted, forecasted and strategic planning results to facilitate decision-making towards the achievement of the budget and strategic plan.
- Maintain and develop various financial models and standard templates for use by all of Finance during the planning process, ensuring quality, accuracy and focused analytic review.
- Prepare OPEX and other Income analysis with comparison (per department and for hospital as a whole).
- Analyze data to ensure that proper accounting procedure have been followed.
- Responsible in preparation of Quarterly reports to SEC and MPIC.
- Handle the reports needed by Philippine Statistics office regarding Financial Statements.
- Responsible for performing special projects assigned by the management.
- Responsible in completing the Budget report, and send such to Management.
- Perform task that may be needed during the yearly audit.
- Primarily responsible in preparation of Draft Financial Statement for SGV.
- Primarily responsible in completing all the requirements needed by SEC and SGV during and after the Audit of Financial Statements.
- Handles any task that may be assigned from time to time.
