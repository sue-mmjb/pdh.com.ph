Parenting 101: Because Every Child Deserves the Best Start!
On March 17, 2025, Parañaque Doctors Hospital (PDH) hosted an insightful Parenting 101 Talk, led by Dr. Maria Theresa De Jesus, Pediatrician, as part of our Family Health Awareness series.
This event was designed to equip parents and caregivers with essential knowledge on raising happy, healthy children while making the learning process engaging and interactive.
Empowering Parents, Nurturing Families
Parenting is a lifelong journey filled with love, challenges, and learning. During the talk, Dr. De Jesus guided attendees through key topics, including:
- Common Parenting Myths – Separating fact from fiction in child-rearing.
- Child Nutrition and Health – What every parent should know about feeding and caring for their little ones.
- Preventive Healthcare – The importance of regular check-ups, vaccinations, and early health screenings.
- Positive Parenting Techniques – How to foster a loving and supportive environment at home.
More Than Just a Talk—A Fun and Interactive Experience!
Because learning should be fun, the event also featured:
- Exciting games that reinforced key parenting lessons.
- Raffle prizes and giveaways to make the experience even more special.
- A supportive community of parents sharing experiences and insights.
The smiles and laughter from our attendees proved that parenting education doesn’t have to be overwhelming—it can be enjoyable, too!
Alagang PDH Ako: Supporting Families Every Step of the Way
At Parañaque Doctors Hospital, we believe that strong families build strong communities. Through events like Parenting 101, we continue to empower parents with the knowledge and tools they need to give their children the best start in life.
A huge thank you to all the parents and caregivers who participated—we admire your dedication to your child’s well-being! Stay tuned for more family-centered health talks and programs at PDH.
Be the parent your child needs. Choose Alagang PDH Ako!
