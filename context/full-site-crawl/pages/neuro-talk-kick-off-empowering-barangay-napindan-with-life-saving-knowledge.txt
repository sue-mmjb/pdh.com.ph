Neuro Talk Kick-Off: Empowering Barangay Napindan with Life-Saving Knowledge
On March 27, 2025, Parañaque Doctors Hospital (PDH) took an important step toward community health empowerment with the Kick-Off: Neuro Talk at Barangay Napindan. This engaging and informative event shed light on one of the most critical health concerns today—stroke awareness and prevention—proving that learning about health can be both educational and enjoyable!
Led by PDH’s expert Neurologist, Dr. Aubrey Punzalan, residents gained valuable insights into stroke warning signs, prevention strategies, and the importance of early detection. Stroke remains a leading cause of disability and death worldwide, but with the right knowledge and proactive lifestyle changes, its risks can be significantly reduced.
More than just a talk, the event was a true community celebration! Interactive games, exciting prizes, and special giveaways made the learning experience fun and engaging, ensuring that every participant left not only informed but also with smiles on their faces.This successful initiative would not have been possible without the warm support of Barangay Chairman Engr. Gerome Michael S. San Pedro, Barangay Secretary Arlene Ricahuerta, and the entire Barangay Napindan Council and, of course, its residents. Their dedication to community well-being played a key role in making this event impactful.
At PDH, we believe that prevention starts with awareness. Through partnerships like this, we remain committed to bringing quality healthcare, expert knowledge, and compassionate service straight to the heart of our communities.
