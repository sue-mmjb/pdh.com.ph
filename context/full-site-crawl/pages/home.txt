Our Network of Care
Parañaque Doctors Hospital (PDH) is a proud member of Metro Pacific Health (MPH), the largest private hospital group in the country.
The 27 hospitals that make up MPH include Makati Medical Center, Cardinal Santos Medical Center, Asian Hospital and Medical Center, Riverside Medical Center, and Davao Doctors Hospital, as well as 33 outpatient care centers, 6 cancer centers, 2 allied health colleges, a centralized laboratory, and several pharmacies across the country – all working together to deliver high-quality compassionate healthcare to Filipinos.
