Accounting Manager
Qualification:
Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field (e.g., Business Administration).
CPA (Certified Public Accountant) designation is highly preferred or required.
Minimum of 5–7 years of experience in accounting or finance, with at least 2–3 years in a supervisory or managerial role. Proven experience in preparing financial reports, budgeting, and managing audits. Experience in managing a team and coordinating with various departments.
Job Description
- Handles the company General Ledger
- In charge in the preparation of Financial Statements (Balance Sheet, Income Statement and its corresponding attachments)
- Assist in the preparation of Reports
- Review and audit journal entries in FMS
- Review withholding tax expanded schedules
- Prepare monthly and quarterly ITR
- Supervise the Accounts Payable section
- Review vouchers and payments to supplier (Trade and Non-Trade)
- Monitor and record monthly accrual of expenses
- Monitor and record monthly amortization of prepayments
- Record manually cost of sales
- Process period and post-closing entries
- Create new/additional income and balance sheet account
- Prepare weekly revenue and admission monitoring
- Primary responsible in year-end audit by SGV
- Responsible in preparing cleared consolidation reporting package at year end
- Assists in the preparation of budget
- Develops, implements and maintains general accounting systems and controls, supervises and participates in the review and analysis of accounting records.
- Encoding of monthly e-sales report
- Prepare inventory list at year end for BIR Submission
- Review transactions requested for cancellation/voiding
- Review and record inventory adjustments
- Handles any task that may be assigned from time to time.
