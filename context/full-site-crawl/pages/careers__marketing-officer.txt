Marketing Officer
Qualifications:
Organizational skills.
Customer service skills.
Technology and software skills.
College Degree Holder.
With Experience in Supervisory.
Basic knowledge in MS excel, powerpoint and fast learner.
Experience in healthcare industry is a plus.
Job Description
The primary role is to promote hospital services to patients and customers, aiming to establish a recognized brand, enhance the hospital’s reputation, and increase company revenue. The position focuses on:
- Attracting attention and generating interest in the hospital’s services.
- Creating and maintaining a strong brand image.
- Implementing marketing strategies to engage, inform, and retain patients.
- Ensure that daily operations, company procedures, and customer service standards are consistently followed.
- Complete sales and marketing administrative tasks, including running marketing reports, conducting research, and maintaining databases.
- Develop promotional materials for hospital services, health packages, and special offers.
- Manage branding and public relations, including promotional strategies and product development.
- Organize health-related events, sponsorships, and community outreach programs.
- Provide logistical support for events, seminars, and trade shows.
- Assist in writing and editing sales and marketing materials, including articles, presentations, and promotional content.
- Maintain project tracking spreadsheets and business marketing calendars, ensuring all stakeholders execute the Marketing Calendar effectively.
- Act as a liaison between the hospital and advertising agencies, print suppliers, freelance talent, and marketing service providers.
- Prepare event invitations, agendas, track payments or refunds, and registration forms for hospital-led events.
- Provide personal and administrative support to directors, including coordinating travel, scheduling appointments, and preparing agendas.
- Manage marketing and media event budgets, ensuring smooth execution of trade shows and seminars.
- Regularly coordinate and collaborate with other departments to ensure successful execution of marketing initiatives.
- Perform other duties assigned by management as needed.
