PDH Careers
Accounting Staff
Qualification:
Bachelor of Science in Accountancy or Business-Related course and Should have 1-3 years’ experience as Accounting Assistant.
Job Description
Assist in the preparation of tax returns (e.g., VAT, income tax, withholding tax).
File BIR forms and other government compliance reports, either manually or via eFPS/eBIR.
Gather and organize supporting documents such as invoices, official receipts, and BIR Forms 2307.
Reconcile tax reports with accounting records (e.g., sales vs. VAT returns).
Assist in reconciling Expanded Withholding Tax (EWT) and final tax certificates (e.g., Forms 2307 and 2316).
