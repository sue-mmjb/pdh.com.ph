Medical Secretary
Qualification:
Bachelor’s degree in any four-year course; Office Administration or related fields preferred. Experience in the healthcare industry is an advantage.
Job Description
- Prepare and edit correspondence , communications, presentations, and other documents of the Chief Medical Officer
- Conduct research, assemble and analyze data to prepare reports and documents.
- File and retrieve documents and reference materials.
- Arrange and coordinate meetings and events of the Medical Director.
- Record, transcribe and distribute minutes of the Meeting
- Answer and manage incoming calls.
- Monitor, respond to, and distribute incoming communications.
- Receive applications and schedule interviews of Medical House Staff (Resident Moonlighters and Junior Consultants).
- Responsible for maintaining and updating Medical Staff 201 files.
- Prepare, maintain and manage timekeeping records of various Departments.
- Audit incentive reports submitted by the Department RODs/Cons.
- Prepare and coordinate activities of all Department (Lay Forum).
- To do other duties or activities that may be assigned by your superior from time to time.
