Laboratory Services
Our laboratory is fully equipped to conduct a wide range of diagnostic tests, including hematology, clinical microscopy, clinical chemistry, microbiology, immunology, and serology. The laboratory staff ensures quick and accurate results, which are essential for effective treatment planning. With a focus on precision and patient safety, PDH’s laboratory services meet the highest standards in the healthcare industry.

CBC w/ Platelet
ESR (Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate)

Prothrombin Time (PT), PTT
Reticulocyte Count
Malarial Smear
Peripheral Blood Smear
Microbiology (Infection Detection)
AFB Stain
Gram Stain & KOH Smear
Culture & Sensitivity (Blood, Urine, Stool, CSF, Sputum, Swab/Discharge)
