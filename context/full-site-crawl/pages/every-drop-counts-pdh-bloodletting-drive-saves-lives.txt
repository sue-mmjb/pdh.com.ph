Every Drop Counts: PDH Bloodletting Drive Saves Lives
On March 26, 2025, Parañaque Doctors Hospital (PDH) hosted a Bloodletting Drive, and it was nothing short of amazing! With the selfless generosity of our donors, we didn’t just collect blood—we gave hope, second chances, and the gift of life to those in need.
A Simple Act, A Life-Saving Impact
Every drop of blood donated has the power to:
- Save lives – Especially for patients in emergency surgeries, cancer treatments, and those battling blood disorders.
- Support the community – Providing hospitals with a steady blood supply for those whoneed it most.
- Boost health – Donors also benefit, as regular blood donation encourages better blood circulation and reduces certain health risks.
Heroes Among Us
A huge congratulations and heartfelt thank you to all our blood donors—you are the true heroes of this event! Your kindness and compassion remind us that even the simplest gestures can have the biggest impact.
Dahil sa Alagang PDH Ako, We Don’t Just Care—We Share Life!
At PDH, we are committed to making healthcare accessible, impactful, and life-changing. This blood drive is just one of the many ways we give back to the community and help save lives.
Be a hero. Be a donor. And share the Alagang PDH. See you at the next one?
