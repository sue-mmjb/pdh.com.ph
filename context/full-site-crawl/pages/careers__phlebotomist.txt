Phlebotomist
Qualification:
BS – Medical Technologist with PRC License
Job Description
- Receives laboratory requests and verifies that all necessary information, particularly the requested laboratory examinations, is correctly indicated.
- Ensures that all laboratory requests received are properly charged.
- Responsible for the extraction of blood samples at the ODP, ER and In Patients using established laboratory techniques.
- Ensures proper labeling of all specimen received and makes separate requisitions for laboratory requests
with multiple procedures for processing to the different sections of the laboratory.
- Assists the Blood Blank staff in bleeding of blood donors.
- Responsible for the monitoring and calibration of glucometers found in each station.
- Responsible for the maintenance and adequacy of extraction materials at the phlebotomy area.
- Performs all other related works that will be assigned to him in the exigency of the service.
- Release results to OUT patients and IN patients.
- To do other duties or activities which my assigned by his/her superior from time to time.
