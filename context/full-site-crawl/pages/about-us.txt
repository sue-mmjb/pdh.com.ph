Parañaque Doctors Hospital (PDH) is a premier Level 2 healthcare institution located in the heart of Parañaque City. It is committed to ensuring the well-being of its community by continuously providing high-quality and affordable healthcare services.
Founded on May 8, 2007, by visionary doctors, PDH officially became a member of the Metro Pacific Health (MPH) Group in 2024 — the largest Philippine private hospital network in the country under the Metro Pacific Investments Corporation (MPIC), led by Mr. Manuel V. Pangilinan.
As member of the MPH network, PDH shares the same goal in providing medical excellence and compassionate care to all our patients.
PDH is a pillar of trust in healthcare for its neighboring community, driven by a deep passion for delivering patient-centered care across generations. With a comprehensive range of medical facilities—including a 94-bed inpatient facility, Emergency Room (ER), Intensive Care Unit (ICU), Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU), Operating Room (OR), Delivery Room (DR), Laboratory, and Radiology, PDH is also accredited by the Department of Health (DOH), PhilHealth, and various Health Maintenance Organizations (HMOs), ensuring accessible healthcare and full compliance with government-regulated standards.
More than just a healthcare provider, PDH is a steadfast partner in the community’s journey to wellness.

Our Barangay Partners
Building Healthier Communities, Together. At PDH, we believe that meaningful healthcare starts at the grassroots. Through our strong collaborations with barangay leaders and local communities, we bring essential health education, medical services, and support closer to the people. These partnerships are the cornerstone of our mission to make quality healthcare more accessible, inclusive, and impactful for all.
Barangay Don Bosco
We’re thrilled to partner with Barangay Don Bosco! Through our Memorandum of Agreement, we are committed to improving healthcare access for the community. Just present your Barangay ID or any valid ID to enjoy exclusive PDH benefits.
