PDH Careers
HMO Staff
Qualification:
Must be a graduate of any 4 year course and computer literate.
Job Description
- Must provide efficient customer service to patients.
- Report daily census of processed LOA of procedures and consultations.
- Also assist HMO Liaison officers on any concerns, both operational and administrative.
- Assist in verifying patient’s coverages and HMO details.
- Assist patients at the window for any inquiries, both consultation and procedure.
- Verify patient’s member status.
- Call up HMO providers for outpatient approvals/ inquiries.
- Update doctors list on-leave for the day or on vacation.
- Answer telephone inquiries regarding any concerns related to HMO.
- Verify available doctors per schedule at information department.
- Check availability of forms per HMO provider and coordinate with them for request.
- Must collaborate and coordinate with other departments to be able to process LOA requests.
- To do other related duties or activities which may be assigned by his/her superior from time to time.
