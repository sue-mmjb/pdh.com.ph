ECHO Tech
Qualification:
Graduate of Radiologic Technology with PRC License. Preferably with Echo Training.
Job Description
- Preparing patients for echocardiographic procedures.
- Operating and maintaining ultrasound or echocardiography equipment.
- Capturing high-quality images of the heart and surrounding structures.
- Providing preliminary analysis and findings to physicians.
- Ensuring patient comfort and safety during procedures.
- Keeping accurate and detailed patient records.
- Collaborating with cardiologists and other healthcare professionals.
- Assisting physicians during complex echocardiographic examinations.
