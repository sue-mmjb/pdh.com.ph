Neuro Talk Kick-Off: Empowering Barangay Napindan with Life-Saving Knowledge
On March 27, 2025, Parañaque Doctors Hospital (PDH) took an important step toward community health empowerment with the Kick-Off: Neuro Talk at Barangay Napindan. This engaging and informative event shed light on one of the most critical health concerns today—stroke awareness and prevention—proving that learning about health can be both educational and enjoyable!
