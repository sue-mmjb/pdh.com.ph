IM Family Health Talk Kick-Off: Building Stronger Families, One Health Talk at a Time
On March 24, 2025, Parañaque Doctors Hospital (PDH) proudly launched its IM Family Health Talk in Barangay Upper Bicutan, led by Dr. Keith Darryl Deo Grafil. This initiative was designed to empower families with essential health knowledge while ensuring that learning remains interactive, engaging, and fun!
With summer just around the corner, staying informed about preventive healthcare measures and debunking common medical myths is more important than ever. Residents gained valuable insights into disease prevention, with a strong focus on dengue awareness and healthy lifestyle habits—helping them make well-informed choices for themselves and their families.
But who says learning about health has to be all serious? The event was packed with energy, excitement, and fun! Through interactive discussions, engaging games, and exciting prizes, participants not only learned vital health information but also enjoyed every moment of it. The joy and laughter shared throughout the day proved that staying healthy can be both educational and entertaining!
PDH extends its deepest gratitude to Honorable Francis Sunga (Barangay Chairman), Barangay Secretary Angie Libutan, Barangay Admin Rima B. Trapal, and the entire Barangay Upper Bicutan team for their unwavering support. Your enthusiasm and dedication to community health made this event a resounding success.
At PDH, we believe that knowledge is the foundation of a healthier community—and this is just the beginning! With continued partnerships like this, we look forward to bringing healthcare closer to more communities, ensuring that quality health education is accessible to all.
