PDH Buntis Day 2025: Nurturing Mothers, Strengthening Communities
On March 10, 2025, Parañaque Doctors Hospital (PDH) celebrated Buntis Day, a special gathering dedicated to expectant mothers and their journey to motherhood. This event, filled with education, support, and heartfelt moments, reaffirmed PDH’s commitment to providing expert maternal care with a compassionate touch.
With the guidance of our esteemed OB-GYN specialists—Dr. Ada Angela Cabrera, Dr. Emilyn Areglado, and Dr. Anna Lyn Magbuhos—attendees gained essential knowledge on prenatal care, safe labor and delivery, postpartum wellness, breastfeeding, and informed family planning choices. These discussions empowered soon-to-be moms with the confidence and information they need for a healthy and safe pregnancy.
Beyond medical insights, the event was made even more memorable with the Ms. Buntis Day Pageant, where mothers proudly shared their unique pregnancy experiences. Their inspiring stories of strength, resilience, and love resonated deeply with everyone present, making the celebration even more meaningful.
This remarkable day would not have been possible without the unwavering support of Barangay Don Bosco and Barangay Marcelo Green. We extend our gratitude to Barangay Chairman Mar Jimenez and Barangay Chairman Ian V. Aguilar for their dedication to the well-being of their communities. Their partnership with PDH highlights the power of collaboration in making quality healthcare more accessible to all.
At PDH, we believe that motherhood is a journey best taken with care, guidance, and community support. Thank you to everyone who joined us in celebrating this special day.
Dito lang ‘yan sa PDH—where every mother and baby receive the best care possible!
