Every Drop Counts: PDH Bloodletting Drive Saves Lives
On March 26, 2025, Parañaque Doctors Hospital (PDH) hosted a Bloodletting Drive, and it was
nothing short of amazing! With the selfless generosity of our donors, we didn’t just collect blood—
we gave hope, second chances, and the gift of life to those in need.
