Alagang PDH Barangay Partnership
At Parañaque Doctors Hospital (PDH), we believe that quality healthcare should be accessible, affordable, and tailored to the needs of our community. Through the Alagang PDH Barangay Partnership, we are strengthening our commitment to serving you better by collaborating with local barangays to bring exclusive healthcare benefits right to your neighborhood!
Exclusive Discounts for Our Ka-Barangay!
Your health is our priority! As part of this special partnership, residents of participating barangays can enjoy 5% discounts on Laboratory and Room Fees—ensuring quality care at a more affordable price. No hassle, no fuss—just trusted medical services from your healthcare partner, PDH.
How to Avail?
It’s easy! Simply present your Barangay ID along with any valid government-issued ID that shows your name and address at PDH.
Reminders:
- Senior Citizens and PWDs—only the highest discount applies.
- Discount is valid for cash payments only.
Partnering with Barangays for a Healthier Tomorrow
The Alagang PDH Barangay Partnership is more than just discounts. It is a commitment to building a healthier and stronger community. Through our partnerships, we will also conduct medical missions, health education programs, and wellness initiatives to ensure that every resident has access to essential healthcare services.
Barangays Under the PDH Partnership:
- Barangay Don Bosco
- Barangay Sun Valley
- Barangay Marcelo Green
- Barangay San Antonio
- Barangay Upper Bicutan
- Barangay Napindan
- Latest Addition: Barangay Moonwalk
Dahil sa PDH, Alagang PDH Ako!
Stay tuned for more updates as we continue to expand our barangay partnerships and bring healthcare closer to you. Because at PDH, we don’t just care—we make sure you feel it!
