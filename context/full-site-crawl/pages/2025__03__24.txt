IM Family Health Talk Kick-Off: Building Stronger Families, One Health Talk at a Time
On March 24, 2025, Parañaque Doctors Hospital (PDH) proudly launched its IM Family Health Talk in Barangay Upper Bicutan, led by Dr. Keith Darryl Deo Grafil. This initiative was designed to empower families with essential health knowledge while ensuring that learning remains interactive, engaging, and fun!
