Accounts Receiveable
Job Description
- Maintain customer accounts: Maintain records of customer accounts and ensure accurate records.
- Generate invoices: Create invoices and account statements.
- Collect payments: Collect payments from customers and post receipts.
- Reconcile accounts: Reconcile accounts receivable ledgers with general ledgers.
- Resolve discrepancies: Resolve discrepancies between customer payments and invoices.
- Produce reports: Create monthly financial reports.
- Investigate inquiries: Investigate and resolve irregularities or inquiries.
- Support audits: Support internal and external audits.
- Apply credit practices: Apply fair credit practices.
